# Product

**Макстехнолоджи** (maxtechnology.ru) is the marketing website for a Russian IT development studio. The site presents the company's services, portfolio of cases, tech stack, and provides a contact form for project inquiries.

## Purpose
- Showcase the company's expertise and completed projects
- Generate leads via a contact form that sends submissions to a Telegram bot
- Provide legal/company information (requisites, privacy policy, data processing agreement)

## Audience
Russian-speaking B2B clients looking for a full-cycle software development partner.

## Key Pages
- `/` — Landing page with Hero, Cases, About, Expertise, Stack, and Contact sections
- `/cases` — Portfolio of completed projects
- `/services` — Services offered
- `/contacts` — Contact page
- `/requisites`, `/privacy`, `/agreement` — Legal/company pages

## Content Language
All user-facing content is in **Russian**. Code, comments, and variable names are in English.
